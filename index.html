3 Art Direction

Google Flow / Nano Banana 2 — getting maximum realism

  • Attach the rug photo. Upload a clean, frontal, evenly lit photo of the carpet as a reference image and keep the fidelity line in the prompt. Nano Banana 2 follows reference designs extremely well.
  • One scene per prompt. Don't merge two shots; generate the full shot list as separate runs.
  • Re-roll with seeds. If composition is right but texture is soft, regenerate and append: "increase micro-detail in the carpet pile, individual knots visible."
  • Upscale last. Generate at the campaign aspect ratio, then use Flow's upscale for print.
  • Fix faces second. If the rug is perfect but the model isn't, use a region edit ("change only the model's face…") rather than re-rolling the whole frame.
