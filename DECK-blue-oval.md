## Slide 1/7 - The Arrival (hook)

SLIDE 1 — THE HOOK. The hall stands silent and dark; the sun shaft cuts diagonally across the dusty stone floor. The Persian rug is frozen mid-unroll, rolling open toward the camera through the beam of light — the unrolled half already blazing with colour inside the light, the still-rolled half in shadow, fringe lifting slightly with the motion, a fine swirl of dust rising around it. No model in this frame. The image must make the viewer need to see what unrolls next.
